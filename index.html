What makes a PhoneRadar review different?

We don't want reviews to sound like a specification sheet. A useful review should answer normal questions: Is it comfortable to use? Does the battery last? Is the software annoying? Is the camera actually good in everyday photos? And, most importantly, is it worth the money?
